The glacier survey began in March. Crews measured ice thickness at forty sites. Weather delayed two flights over the ridge. The survey results show steady thinning. Thinning is fastest near the southern edge. Local guides reported fresh meltwater channels. The crew packed camp on the final Friday. A second survey is planned for next year. Funding for the survey comes from regional councils. Glacier thinning matters to every valley below.
