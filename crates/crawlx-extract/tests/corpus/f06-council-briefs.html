Council Approves Riverside Cycle Path

Published Wednesday 14 May, 17:40

Eastgate council voted eleven to four on Wednesday night to build a two mile cycle path along the south bank of the river, closing a gap that has divided the town's two main parks for decades.

The path will be three metres wide and lit from dusk, with a separate strip for walkers. Work is expected to start in September and finish before the following spring, subject to a final check on flood defences.

Councillor Tomas Verhoeven, who proposed the scheme, said it would give children a safe route to the secondary school. Opponents raised the cost, which has risen from 1.8 million to 2.6 million pounds since the first estimate.

The money will come from a regional transport grant and from the sale of a depot site on Anchor Lane, so no increase in council tax is planned.

Residents can inspect the final drawings at the library until the end of June.
