Ferry Crews Test the New Quiet Engines

  • Email this
  • Print page
  • Copy link

Two of the three Bayside ferries now run on electric drive units, and on Monday the crews took one of them across the bay in the first full test with passengers aboard. The engines are so quiet that the captain used a hand bell at the dock.

Passengers noticed the difference at once. Seats near the engine room used to hum, and now people can hold a conversation without raising their voices. Engineer Sunniva Rask says the real gain is for the crew, who spend twelve hours a day near the machinery.

Battery charging takes place at the north dock between crossings. The harbour authority has installed a second cable so that both ferries can charge at the same time during the rush hour.

If the trial goes well, the third ferry will be converted over the winter. The company says tickets will not change in price, though it hopes to lower its fuel bill by a third.
