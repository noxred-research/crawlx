Free Bike Repair Clinic Returns to the Station Square

Sponsored: Cycle Palace summer sale, up to half off

The monthly bike repair clinic returns to Station Square on Saturday, with volunteer mechanics offering free checks of brakes, tires and chains. Last month they looked at more than ninety bicycles in four hours.

Visitors can bring a bike with any problem, from a squeaky pedal to a bent wheel. Small parts are paid for by a neighborhood fund, and larger repairs are explained so owners can do them at home.

The clinic runs from ten in the morning until two in the afternoon. In case of rain it moves into the empty ticket hall.
