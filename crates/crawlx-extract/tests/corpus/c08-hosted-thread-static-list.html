Building a Rain Barrel Stand

A full rain barrel weighs more than two hundred kilograms, so the stand underneath it matters more than the barrel itself. This one is made from six treated posts, eight cross braces and a plywood top, and it took me one afternoon.

Set the posts on concrete slabs, not directly on soil, and check that they are level in both directions before you screw anything together. A tilted stand will creep further out of true every time the barrel fills.

Raise the barrel high enough that a watering can fits under the tap. Thirty centimetres is plenty, and it lets gravity do the work.
