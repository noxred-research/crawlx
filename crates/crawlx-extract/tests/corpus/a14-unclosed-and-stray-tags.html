Allotment Waiting List Falls Below Two Hundred

The waiting list for council allotments has dropped below two hundred names for the first time in nine years, after the parks department opened thirty new plots on the old rail embankment.

Officials say the average wait is now fourteen months, down from three years. Gardeners on the longest-waiting list were offered the first choice of plots, and most accepted within a week.

Each new plot comes with a water tap shared between four neighbours, a locked shed for tools and a compost bay. Rent is set at thirty euros a year, with half price for people over sixty-five.

The parks department has promised to review the list again in September. Anyone who no longer wants a plot is asked to tell the office so that the next person can be called.
