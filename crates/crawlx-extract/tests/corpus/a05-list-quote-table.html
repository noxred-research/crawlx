Market Stall Fees Change in the New Season

The covered market will adjust its stall fees on the first of March, bringing the weekday rate closer to the weekend rate. Traders voted on the change during their autumn meeting.

  • Weekday stalls: 9 euros per day
  • Weekend stalls: 12 euros per day
  • Seasonal pass: 180 euros for the quarter
“The new rates are fairer to people who sell only a few days a week," said trader Tomas Brandt.

Traders who already hold a seasonal pass keep their current price until the pass expires. The market office will mail updated receipts at the end of February.
