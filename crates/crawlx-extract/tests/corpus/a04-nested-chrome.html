Chess Club Reopens in the Old Post Office

The Millbrook chess club has reopened in the old post office, where members found a hall large enough for twelve boards and a demonstration wall. The club had met in a church basement for nine years before the landlord sold the building.

Share this story: Mail Link Print

Membership is free for anyone under eighteen, and beginners can borrow sets from the front desk. Coach Idris Vale runs an open lesson at six each Wednesday that covers one opening idea and one endgame trick.
