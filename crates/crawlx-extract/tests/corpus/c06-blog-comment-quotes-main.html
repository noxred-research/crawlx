Why Our Library Needs a Reading Garden

The courtyard behind the library has been locked for six years, filled with broken paving and weeds. A reading garden would turn it into the only public outdoor room in the village, free to use and open whenever the library is.

Our proposal costs less than a new roof for the bicycle shed. Volunteers will lay a gravel path, plant a border of lavender and install four benches made from reclaimed timber by the school woodwork club.

Nothing in this plan requires paid maintenance. Local gardeners have promised to look after the beds on a rota, and the parish council has agreed to cover the water bill for the first three years.
