Introducing the Ashlar Commuter

After two years of testing on cobbles, tram lines and wet leaves, we are releasing the Ashlar Commuter, a steel framed city bike built for people who ride every day and do not want to think about it.

The frame is made from heat treated steel tubing with internal cable routing, which keeps the lines clean and the cables out of the weather. A belt drive replaces the chain, so there is nothing to oil and nothing to stain a trouser leg.

Mudguards, a rear rack and dynamo lights are fitted as standard. The eight speed hub gearbox is sealed against rain, and the brakes are hydraulic discs that work the same in a downpour as on a dry day.

The Ashlar will be available in three sizes and two colours from the first of March. Pre-orders open today, and every bike ships with a free first service at any Greywheel store.
