A Street Festival in Three Languages

The annual Crossroads festival closed Maple Avenue on Saturday, and the loudest sound was not music but conversation. Stallholders greeted shoppers in the language they heard first, and most customers answered in kind.

« Ici, on dit bonjour avant de dire le prix », a expliqué la boulangère Clémence Aubry en riant.

Her neighbour, the grocer Mateo Reyes, agreed. «La gente vuelve por el pan y se queda por la charla», dijo mientras pesaba limones.

At the end of the street a tea merchant had hung a banner of her own. 欢迎光临，请先尝一杯茶。 It means welcome, please try a cup of tea first.

Organizers counted nine thousand visitors and say the festival will return in June, with a bigger stage for storytelling in every language the neighbourhood speaks.
