Best way to dry hops at home?

pilsner_pete

I grew a few bines this year and picked about two kilos of cones last weekend. What is the best way to dry them without a proper kiln? I do not want to lose the aroma.

malt_marta

Spread them thinly on a mesh rack in a dark, airy room. Mine take three or four days. Turn them once a day and they are ready when the stem snaps instead of bending.

kettle_kev

A fan heater on its lowest setting works too, but keep the temperature under forty degrees or you will drive off the oils.

Vacuum seal them afterwards and freeze.

pilsner_pete

Thanks both. I will try the mesh rack in the spare bedroom and report back in a week.
