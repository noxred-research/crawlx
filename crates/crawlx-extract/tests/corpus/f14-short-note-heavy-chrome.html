Comet Tail Spotted Over the Northern Hills

Amateur observers in the northern hills photographed the tail of a faint comet on Monday night, about two hours after sunset, low above the treeline.

The comet is not bright enough to see without binoculars, but long exposures show a pale tail more than three degrees long. Observers recommend a dark site and a clear view toward the north west.

It will fade over the coming week as it moves away from the sun.
