Lumen Slate 6 Review: A Reader That Gets Out of the Way

The Lumen Slate 6 is a six inch e-ink reader with a frosted glass screen, a physical page-turn rocker and a battery that lasted five weeks in our daily test. It is not trying to do anything clever, and that is its strength.

The screen is sharp enough that serif text looks printed, and the front light warms smoothly toward amber in the evening. We read two novels in a dim bedroom without once noticing the backlight.

Specifications

Screen6 inch, 300 ppi
Storage16 GB
Battery2000 mAh
Weight172 g

Using it every day

Page turns take a little under a quarter of a second, and a full refresh happens every ten pages by default. You can change that to every page if ghosting bothers you.

It is the first reader I have owned that I forgot about while reading.

Transferring books is where the Slate shows its age. There is no wireless store, only a cable and a drag and drop folder, which suits us but may frustrate newcomers.

Verdict

If you want a simple, durable reader for novels and long articles, the Slate 6 is an easy recommendation. If you read comics or technical manuals, look at a larger screen.
