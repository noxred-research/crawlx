A Short Glossary of Tide Terms

Anyone who walks the flats at low water should know a handful of words. These definitions come from the harbour pilots who taught the local sailing school.

Neap tide
A tide with a small range, which happens when the moon is at its first or last quarter.
Spring tide
A tide with a large range, which follows the new and the full moon.
Slack water
The short time when the water neither rises nor falls.

Common questions

Why are there two high tides a day?

The moon pulls the sea on its near side and, in effect, leaves a bulge on the far side too, so most coasts see two highs and two lows in a little over a day.

Is slack water the safest time to swim?

It is often calmer, but local currents can start without warning, so swimmers should check the harbour chart first.
