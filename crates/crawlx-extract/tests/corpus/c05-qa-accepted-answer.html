How do I stop a door from sticking in summer?

The bathroom door in our flat swells in warm weather and scrapes on the frame. In winter it is perfect. I would rather not plane it. Is there a lasting way to fix this?

12 votes
Is the door painted on all four edges? Dalia, 4 hours ago
Only the faces. The top and bottom are bare wood. Ivo, 3 hours ago
Accepted answer

Bare edges soak up moisture from the air, which is why the door swells. Take it off its hinges, sand the top and bottom lightly and seal them with two coats of exterior varnish. Rehang it and the problem should not come back.
