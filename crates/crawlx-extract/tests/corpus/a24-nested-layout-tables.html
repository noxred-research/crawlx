The Village Hall Roof Fund

The parish has raised sixty percent of the money needed to replace the village hall roof, after a bring-and-buy sale on Saturday added more than four thousand euros to the total.

The old tiles have been leaking above the stage since the autumn, and the badminton club has already moved its games to the school gym. A builder's survey says the timbers underneath are still sound.

Volunteers will run a quiz night next month and a sponsored walk in the spring. Anyone wishing to give can leave an envelope at the post office or ask the treasurer, Edda Pryce, for a form.
