Dockside Hold Out for a Replay

Dockside Rovers came from behind twice at Millbrook Park on Saturday to earn a 2-2 draw in the third round of the county cup, and the tie will now be settled at their own ground on Tuesday evening.

Millbrook went ahead after eleven minutes when Joss Randall turned in a corner at the near post. Dockside equalised just before the break through a long range shot from midfielder Aiden Kaur that dipped under the bar.

The home side restored their lead early in the second half, but Rovers substitute Lena Marsh headed in a free kick with seven minutes left to rescue the draw.

Result

Millbrook Town2
Dockside Rovers2

The winner will play the holders in the fourth round on the first weekend of December.
