New Ferry Terminal Opens at Gannet Quay

The new ferry terminal at Gannet Quay welcomed its first passengers on Thursday morning, replacing a wooden shelter that had served the island crossing since the nineteen sixties.

The building has a heated waiting hall, a ticket office, a small cafe and covered access to both vehicle decks. A level walkway means wheelchair users no longer need help to board.

The operator said the terminal cost nine million pounds and was paid for jointly by the island trust and a coastal regeneration fund. Crossings continue every ninety minutes.
