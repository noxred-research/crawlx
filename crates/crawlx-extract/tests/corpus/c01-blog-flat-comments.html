Mending a Wool Jumper Without Anyone Noticing

A small moth hole in a good jumper does not have to mean the end of it. With a matching thread and a darning needle you can close the gap in about twenty minutes, and the repair will survive many washes.

Start by turning the jumper inside out and finding yarn from the hem or the inside of a seam. Wool thread taken from the garment itself will always match, because it has aged in the same way as the rest of the fabric.

Weave the needle in a grid across the hole, first in one direction and then at right angles, catching a few intact stitches at every edge. Do not pull the thread tight. Leave the patch slightly loose so that it can relax into the knit when the jumper is steamed.
