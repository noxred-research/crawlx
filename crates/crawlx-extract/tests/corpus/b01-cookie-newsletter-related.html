Ferry Timetable Changes on the Northern Route

The northern ferry route will run a revised timetable from the second week of April, with the first departure moving from half past six to a quarter to seven. The operator said the change matches the new tide tables published by the harbor authority.

Two evening sailings will be added on Fridays, a response to requests from restaurant owners on the island. The last boat back from the mainland will leave at ten forty, giving diners enough time to finish their meals.

Passengers with monthly tickets do not need to change anything. Printed timetables will be available at the terminal kiosk from the first of April.
