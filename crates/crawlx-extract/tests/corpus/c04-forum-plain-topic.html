Wireless network drops every evening

brook_lane

Every evening around seven my wireless connection disappears for about ten minutes and then comes back by itself. The router is two years old and the cable is fine. Has anyone seen this before?

nettle42

Check which channel the router is on. Seven in the evening is when everybody in a block of flats switches on their streaming boxes, and the crowded channels fall over.

brook_lane

It was on channel six, shared with at least nine other networks. I moved to channel one hundred and four and the drops have stopped.

solder_sam

Worth marking as solved. Some routers pick a congested channel on every restart, so set it by hand.

Also check for firmware updates.
