Twelve Ways to Cut Winter Heating Costs

A cold house does not have to mean a large bill. These twelve changes cost little and most can be done in a weekend.

  1. Bleed the radiators. Trapped air leaves the top of a radiator cold, so release it once at the start of the season.
  2. Close the curtains at dusk. Thick fabric holds heat that would otherwise leak through the glass.
  3. Seal the gaps. A tube of flexible filler around window frames pays for itself within one winter.
  4. Add foil behind radiators. Reflective sheets push heat into the room and away from the outside wall.
  5. Lower the thermostat by one degree. Most people never notice, and the saving can reach a tenth of the bill.
  6. Use a draught excluder. A rolled towel at the base of the door works nearly as well as a bought one.
  7. Service the boiler. A yearly check keeps it efficient and finds small faults before they grow.
  8. Move furniture off radiators. A sofa in front of a heater blocks warm air from reaching the room.
  9. Insulate the hot water tank. A fitted jacket cuts heat loss and costs about the price of a pizza.
  10. Cover bare floors. Rugs on wooden boards stop cold rising through the gaps.
  11. Heat the person, not the house. A warm jumper and a blanket make a lower temperature comfortable.
  12. Check your tariff. Compare offers every year, because prices change and loyalty is rarely rewarded.
