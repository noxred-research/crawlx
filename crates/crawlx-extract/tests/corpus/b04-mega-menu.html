Tram Stop Renamed After the Old Cannery

The tram stop at the end of Dock Street will be renamed Old Cannery from the start of next month. Signs, maps and announcements will change on the same day.

The name recalls the fish cannery that stood on the site until the nineteen sixties. A local history group asked for the change after finding the original tram timetable in an attic.

City Council. Open Monday to Friday. Accessibility statement.
