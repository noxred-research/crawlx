Tidewater Library Opens Night Reading Room

The Tidewater Library opened a night reading room on Thursday, giving residents a quiet place to study until ten o'clock. Librarians moved forty chairs and six long tables into the former map archive over the weekend.

The idea came from a survey of shift workers, who said the regular opening hours rarely matched their schedules. About two hundred people answered, and most asked for evening access to printers, outlets and a warm room.

Staff will review attendance after three months. If the room stays busy, the council may fund a second evening on Sundays, according to the library board's planning notes.
