Workshop Notes: Building a Bat Box A bat box takes one afternoon, a single plank and basic tools. Fix it three metres above ground on a wall that gets sun for most of the day, & keep the entrance facing south.

Cut the plank into one back panel, one front panel and two sides.

The back should be longer than the front so that the bats have a landing strip.

Materials
  • One untreated plank, 1.2 metres long
  • Twenty galvanised screws
  • A strip of rough mesh for the landing

Measurements
Back panel40 cm
Front panel30 cm
Roof25 cm

Never paint the inside of the box. Bats avoid fresh paint, and rough wood gives them something to grip. R&D on the topic shows that a gap of two centimetres is the most popular.

Weekend Workshop. Safety first: always wear eye protection.
