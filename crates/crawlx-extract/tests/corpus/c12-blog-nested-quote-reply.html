Three Weeks Without a Phone Alarm

For three weeks I woke up to a plain mechanical clock with a bell the size of a walnut. It was loud, unreasonable and impossible to snooze, and by the second morning I had stopped fighting it.

The biggest surprise was not the earlier mornings but the quieter evenings. Once the phone no longer slept beside my bed, I stopped reaching for it at ten at night, and my sleep got deeper without any other change.

I have gone back to the phone for travel, but the clock stays on the shelf for ordinary weeks.
