The final rounds

By the winter of 1958 only two lamplighters were left in the old town. Anselm Greaves walked the north streets and a woman known only as Mrs Carr took the market lanes, each carrying a long pole with a small brass hook.

Their rounds began at dusk. A lamp had to be lit, checked and left burning, then turned off again at dawn, and the work was done in all weathers. Greaves once lit forty lamps in a snowstorm with a scarf tied across his face.

When the electric switch came, the council held a small ceremony. The last gas lamp on Cobbler's Row was put out by Mrs Carr, who kept her pole and hung it above her door.
