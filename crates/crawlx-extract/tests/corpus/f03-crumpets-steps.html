Sourdough Crumpets From Discard

Crumpets are the ideal home for the sourdough starter you would otherwise throw away. The batter is thin, the holes appear on their own, and a griddle with a few steel rings is all the equipment you need.

Ingredients

  • 250 g sourdough discard, straight from the fridge
  • 150 ml warm milk
  • 1 teaspoon fine salt
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • half a teaspoon of bicarbonate of soda

Method

  1. Whisk the discard with the milk, salt and sugar until smooth. Leave the bowl in a warm corner for forty minutes.
  2. Dissolve the bicarbonate in two tablespoons of warm water and stir it in. The batter will bubble and loosen.
  3. Grease the rings and set them on a medium heat griddle. Pour in about two centimetres of batter.
  4. Cook for six to eight minutes without touching. When the surface is covered in open holes and no longer shiny, lift the rings away.
  5. Flip for one minute to colour the top, then serve at once with plenty of butter.

Leftover crumpets freeze well. Toast them straight from the freezer.
