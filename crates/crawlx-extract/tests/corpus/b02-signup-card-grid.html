How to Keep a Sourdough Starter Alive on Holiday

A sourdough starter can wait for you in the refrigerator for up to three weeks if you feed it well on the day you leave. Stir in equal weights of flour and water, close the jar loosely and put it on the coldest shelf.

When you come back, pour off any dark liquid on top and feed the starter twice at room temperature before baking. It may smell sharp at first, which is normal, and it should bubble again within a day.

If you will be away longer, spread a thin layer of starter on baking paper and let it dry. The flakes keep for months and wake up after a warm bath of flour and water.
